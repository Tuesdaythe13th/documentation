ARTIFEX Labs is a multilingual compositional safety evaluation suite that benchmarks AI content-safety pipelines across languages, dialects, and cultural contexts. Built around a formal 2026 measurement science framework, it combines production-grade Python CLI tools with self-contained Google Colab notebooks to produce auditable, standards-aligned safety evaluations. Whether you are stress-testing a deployed content moderation system or designing a rigorous benchmark for publication, ARTIFEX Labs gives you the measurement infrastructure to do it transparently and reproducibly.

## Who ARTIFEX Labs Is For

ARTIFEX Labs is designed for practitioners who need more than pass/fail accuracy numbers:

- **AI safety engineers** validating content-moderation pipelines before deployment, especially across languages and locales where test coverage is typically thin.
- **Benchmark designers** who need a verifiable supply-chain audit trail (BBOM) and machine-checkable rubric gates at every decision point in the pipeline.
- **Researchers** studying cross-lingual value alignment, dialect divergence, or cultural harm categories — the suite ships a 10-dimension cultural taxonomy and dialect datasets out of the box.
- **ML teams** running continuous integration on safety regressions — the CLI evaluator supports offline stub-model mode and checkpoint/resume for integration into any CI/CD workflow.

## Key Capabilities

**Multilingual evaluation across languages and dialects.** The core evaluator uses `paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2` (100+ languages) and supports ensemble models such as RoBERTuito and multilingual-E5-Large. Dialect-level benchmarks cover Colombian Spanish, Castilian vs. Mexican Spanish, and US/UK/AU English, with BCP-47 language tagging throughout.

**Adaptive Boolean Rubrics.** Every pipeline decision point is governed by a `BooleanRubricResult` dataclass — a machine-checkable, auditable gate that records the gate name, pass/fail outcome, confidence score, severity level, and raw evidence. Eleven rubric gates span dataset quality, embedding ensemble readiness, clustering stability, judge calibration, jury routing, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) triplet availability.

**LLM-as-Judge with bias correction.** The suite uses calibrated LLM judges with bias-corrected θ̂ estimators and Pydantic structured outputs. Multi-judge majority voting (JSR/CCR metrics) is demonstrated in the Spanish AILuminate notebook across 500 jailbreak prompts.

**Cultural harm taxonomy.** A 10-dimension `cultural_taxonomy.yaml` covers religious sensitivity, social norm violation, political content, historical trauma, language-specific taboo, hate, self-harm, violence, explicit content, and a generic fallback — each with geographic context, severity rating, and annotation examples.

**Google Colab notebooks.** Eleven self-contained notebooks cover everything from a pedagogical evaluator design walkthrough to a full end-to-end safety pipeline. Each notebook includes a 2026 Measurement Science cell mapping its methodology to NIST AI 800-3 GLMMs, IRT 3PL construct validity, and the five facets of validity.

**CLI tools.** Two production-grade Python scripts — `evaluator.py` and `ingest.py` — handle high-throughput scoring and data ingestion respectively. Both scripts support checkpoint/resume, strict-mode CI exits, and offline operation.

## Standards Alignment

ARTIFEX Labs aligns with three formal standards:

| Standard | Version | ARTIFEX Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **NIST AI 800-3** | 2026 | GLMM variance decomposition, benchmark vs. generalised accuracy |
| **ISO/IEC 42119-2/3** | Oct 2025 | Testing techniques and verification & validation across the AI lifecycle |
| **MLCommons AILuminate** | 2026 | Structured risk assurance and BBOM supply-chain audit |
